## I. Potential Hazards

A. Low overhead obstructions and/or congested work area could result in lacerations, concussion, and/or contusions.

B. Lacerations, contusions, and/or abrasions could result due to striking surrounding structures or equipment.

C. Severe crush injury and/or amputation could result from unexpected operation of moving parts of pump.

D. Severe lacerations and/or amputation could result from unexpected operation of moving parts of pump.

E. Burns, skin/eye irritation, and/or temporary respiratory problems could result from contact with substance discharged through pump.

F. Flying objects/debris could result in lacerations, eye injuries, or injuries from embedded objects.

G. Slips and falls could result due to wet and/or uneven working surfaces.

H. Slips and falls could result from improper housekeeping practices.
