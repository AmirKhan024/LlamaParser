## Definitions

- **Claim** — a request for reimbursement of one or more business expenses, submitted through the expense system, made up of one or more supporting **documents**.
- **Employee** — a permanent employee of Konkan Digital Systems Private Limited.
- **Reporting Manager** — the employee's direct manager as recorded in HRMS.
- **Grade** — the employee's job level, L1 through L6 (§2.1).
- **City Tier** — the cost-of-living classification of an Indian city, Tier 1/2/3 (§2.2).
- **Zone** — the classification of an international destination, Zone A/B (§2.3).
- **Travel Desk** — Konkan Digital's empanelled corporate travel booking service.
- **Business day** — a calendar day that is not a weekend or a Konkan Digital-observed holiday.

---

## 1. Scope and Principles

1.1. This policy governs reimbursement of expenses genuinely and reasonably incurred by an employee in the course of Konkan Digital business — client work, internal meetings, training, and approved travel. It does not create an entitlement to spend up to any stated cap; caps are ceilings, not targets.

1.2. **Business purpose required.** Every claim must state a genuine business purpose. An expense with no identifiable business purpose is not reimbursable, regardless of amount or approval level.

1.3. **Spend as if it were your own money.** Employees are expected to exercise the same judgment and care they would apply to their own funds — choosing reasonable, not lavish, options.

1.4. **Lowest reasonable cost.** Where multiple reasonable options exist (fare class, hotel category, vendor), the employee should choose the lowest-cost option that reasonably meets the business need, not the maximum the policy permits.

1.5. **No personal expenses.** Expenses for personal benefit, convenience unrelated to business need, or for family/dependents are not reimbursable under any clause of this policy (see also §19).

1.6. Nothing in this policy overrides statutory tax withholding or reporting obligations; Finance may adjust the taxability of a reimbursement independently of whether it is approved for payment.

## 3. Air Travel

3.1. All air travel should be booked through the Travel Desk wherever possible; the Travel Desk has negotiated corporate fares and handles GST invoicing correctly. Self-booking is permitted when the Travel Desk cannot meet the required schedule, with intimation to the Reporting Manager.

3.2. **Advance booking.** Domestic tickets should be booked at least 7 days in advance; international tickets at least 14 days in advance. Late booking without a documented urgent business reason (e.g., client escalation, force majeure) may result in the claim being reduced to the fare that would have applied at the required advance-booking window.

3.3. **Lowest logical fare.** Employees must select the lowest-cost itinerary within a 2-hour window of the required departure/arrival time, on a reasonably direct routing (at most one reasonable connection for domestic, at most two for international).

3.4. **Class of travel — domestic flights**

| Grade | Class |
|---|---|
| L1 | Economy |
| L2 | Economy |
| L3 | Economy (Economy Plus / extra-legroom permitted for flights over 3 hours) |
| L4 | Economy Plus / Premium Economy |
| L5 | Business permitted for flights over 3 hours; otherwise Economy Plus |
| L6 | Business, any duration |

3.5. **Class of travel — international flights**

| Grade | Under 6 hours | 6 hours and over |
|---|---|---|
| L1 | Economy | Economy |
| L2 | Economy | Economy |
| L3 | Economy | Premium Economy |
| L4 | Premium Economy | Business (Department Head pre-approval required) |
| L5 | Business | Business |
| L6 | Business | Business (First Class at the Executive's discretion, logged as a policy exception per §23.4) |

3.6. **Baggage and seat fees.** One checked bag within the airline's standard allowance is reimbursable for any flight. Extra baggage requires a stated business reason (e.g., client deliverables, demo hardware). Seat-selection fees are reimbursable only for L4 and above on flights over 3 hours; otherwise not reimbursable.

3.7. **Cancellation and change fees.** Reimbursable when the change is driven by a genuine business reason (client reschedule, itinerary change directed by a manager, illness, force majeure) with Reporting Manager approval. Not reimbursable when the change is for personal convenience.

## 5. Local Transport

5.1. **Ride-hailing and cabs — allowed vehicle tier by grade**

| Grade | Allowed tier |
|---|---|
| L1 – L3 | Economy / hatchback-sedan tier (e.g., "Go"/"Mini"-equivalent) |
| L4 – L5 | Sedan / Premium Sedan |
| L6 | Any tier, including premium |

5.2. Premium tiers (SUV, luxury sedan) above an employee's allowed tier are not reimbursable unless no alternative is available — e.g., group travel with colleagues, heavy equipment/luggage, or no lower-tier vehicle available at the time — and the reason is noted on the claim.

5.3. **Per-ride cap**

| City tier / zone | Cap per ride |
|---|---|
| Tier 1 | ₹800 |
| Tier 2 | ₹500 |
| Tier 3 | ₹400 |
| International (Zone A) | $35 |
| International (Zone B) | $25 |

5.3.1. **Airport transfers.** A cab or ride-hailing trip to or from the airport is reimbursable as local transport under this clause, at the applicable per-ride cap; it is a separate expense from the flight itself (§3), which is booked and claimed under intercity travel.

5.3.2. **Late-night travel.** A cab home after 9:00 PM for safety reasons is reimbursable at the standard allowed tier for the employee's grade, regardless of whether the employee would normally use public transport, provided the late finish was for a genuine business reason (client call, release, event).

5.4. Auto-rickshaw, metro, and other public transport fares are reimbursable at actuals with a receipt or a digital payment record (e.g., metro card top-up receipt, UPI payment screenshot showing the vendor).

5.5. Parking and toll charges incurred during business travel are reimbursable at actuals with a receipt.

5.6. **Daily commute excluded.** The employee's regular home-to-office and office-to-home commute is never reimbursable, regardless of grade, except for the late-night safety exception in §5.3.2.

6.5. Mileage and fuel (§7) cannot both be claimed for the same trip — an employee claims one or the other, never both, for a given journey.

## 7. Fuel

7.1. Fuel expenses are reimbursable at actuals, with a fuel station receipt, only for: (a) a company-leased vehicle, or (b) an employee at L5 or above who is on the company car policy.

7.2. Fuel receipts must show the station name, date, quantity, and amount. A card slip alone is not sufficient (§20.3).

7.3. Fuel and mileage (§6) cannot both be claimed for the same trip.

## 9. Meals While Travelling

9.1. Employees may claim meals while travelling either as a fixed daily allowance (per diem, no receipts needed) **or** as actuals up to the same cap (receipts required) — the employee chooses per trip, not per meal, and may not mix both methods on the same day.

9.2. **Per diem / actuals cap**

| City tier / zone | Amount per day |
|---|---|
| Tier 1 | ₹1,200 |
| Tier 2 | ₹900 |
| Tier 3 | ₹700 |
| International Zone A | $70 |
| International Zone B | $50 |

9.3. Alcohol is never reimbursable under travel meals (it is only ever reimbursable under client entertainment, §10.3, within that clause's cap).

9.4. Tips are reimbursable up to 10% of the underlying bill.

## 10. Client Entertainment

10.1. Meals or events with clients or prospects are reimbursable when the business purpose, client company name, and number of attendees are recorded on the claim.

10.2. **Per-person cap:** ₹2,500 (domestic) / $60 (international).

10.3. Alcohol is permitted only under this clause, capped at 30% of the total bill.

10.4. **Pre-approval required** for any single client entertainment event above ₹15,000 (or $200) — obtained from the Reporting Manager before the event.

## 13. Software and Subscriptions

13.1. Any paid software, SaaS tool, or online subscription for business use requires IT pre-approval before purchase.

13.2. Personal subscriptions (streaming, personal cloud storage, personal productivity tools) are never reimbursable (see also §19).

13.3. Annual billing plans require both Reporting Manager and IT approval; monthly billing is the default without this additional approval.

## 14. Office Supplies and Equipment

14.1. Small purchases under ₹2,000 per item (stationery, a mouse, a headset) may be bought and claimed directly without prior approval.

14.2. Any purchase of ₹2,000 or more is capital equipment and must go through Procurement as a company asset — it is not reimbursed as an expense claim.

## 15. Training, Certifications and Conferences

15.1. Any course, certification, exam, or conference requires pre-approval from the Reporting Manager and, for a spend above ₹50,000, from an L5-or-above approver, before enrolment.

15.2. Covered costs: course/exam fees and official course materials. Conference passes are covered under this clause; travel and accommodation to attend are claimed under the relevant travel clauses (§3–§9), not under this one.

15.3. **Service bond.** Any single course or certification costing more than ₹50,000 requires the employee to sign a 12-month service retention bond before enrolment.

## 16. Travel Documents and Fees

16.1. **Visa fees** for an approved business trip are reimbursable at actuals with pre-approval.

16.2. **Passport fees** are reimbursable only when a first-time application or renewal is solely necessitated by a specific approved business trip and the employee holds no other valid passport; capped at the government fee (expedited/tatkal surcharge requires pre-approval).

16.3. **Travel insurance** is mandatory for all international trips — arranged by the Travel Desk where possible, or reimbursed at actuals up to $50/trip.

16.4. **Forex fees.** Currency conversion/markup fees and prepaid forex card loading fees are reimbursable up to 2% of the converted amount.

## 17. Business Gifts

17.1. **Per-recipient limit:** ₹2,500 (domestic) / $50 (international).

17.2. **Anti-bribery.** Gifts to government officials, public-sector employees, or regulators are prohibited without exception, regardless of value, and regardless of local custom.

17.3. Every business gift must be logged in the Gifts Register with recipient name, organization, and occasion, in addition to the expense claim itself.

## 18. Miscellaneous Business Expenses

18.1. Courier/postage and printing for business purposes are reimbursable at actuals. Amounts under ₹1,000 may be self-declared (§20.2); ₹1,000 and above require a receipt.

## 19. Not Reimbursable

The following are never reimbursable, under any clause of this policy:

- Traffic fines, parking fines, and other penalties, however incurred.
- Personal grooming (haircuts, spa, salon) other than the exclusion already noted in §8.4.
- Expenses for family members or other dependents accompanying the employee.
- Purchase or top-up of loyalty points, airline miles, or hotel-status upgrades paid in cash.
- Personal software/media subscriptions (§13.2).
- Alcohol, except within the client entertainment cap (§10.3).
- Entertainment unrelated to business (movies, personal outings, personal gym/club memberships).
- Any expense already reimbursed by a client or under any other Konkan Digital policy or benefit.

## 20. Receipts and Proof

20.1. An itemised original receipt is required for any single expense of ₹500 or more (domestic) or $10 or more (international).

20.2. Below that amount, self-declaration (a short note describing the expense) is acceptable, up to a cumulative ₹3,000 per employee per month across all self-declared expenses.

20.3. A card payment slip or swipe confirmation alone, without an itemised bill, is not accepted as proof of an expense.

20.4. Digital receipts (emailed invoice, PDF, clear photo/scan) are accepted provided they legibly show the vendor name, amount, date, and — where applicable — GSTIN.

---

## 21. Submission Rules

21.1. Domestic expenses must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date.

21.2. Expenses from an international trip must be submitted within 60 days of the employee's return.

21.3. Ordinarily one claim per trip, or one consolidated claim per month for recurring expenses (phone, mileage).

21.4. Claims older than 90 days from the expense date are rejected outright; there is no exception process for a claim submitted this late.

---

## 22. Currency Conversion

22.1. For a card payment, the conversion rate actually applied by the card issuer (as shown on the card/bank statement) is used.

22.2. Where no card statement is available (e.g., cash payment), the Reserve Bank of India reference rate for the transaction date is used.

22.3. For a cash currency exchange, the rate on the money-changer's own receipt is used, provided the receipt is attached.

---

## 23. Approval Matrix

23.1. **Auto-approval.** A claim of ₹5,000 or less with no validation flags (all arithmetic checks pass, no policy-limit breach) is approved automatically.

23.2. **₹5,001 – ₹25,000:** Reporting Manager approval required.

23.3. **₹25,001 – ₹1,00,000:** Reporting Manager and Finance approval required.

23.4. **Above ₹1,00,000:** Reporting Manager, Finance, and Department Head approval required.

23.5. **Policy exceptions.** Any claim that breaches a specific clause of this policy (an over-cap hotel night, a higher class of air travel than the grade allows, etc.) requires a written justification from the employee and approval one level above what the claim amount alone would require under §23.2–23.4.

---

## 24. Compliance

24.1. **Duplicate claims** — submitting the same receipt or bill more than once, in the same or a different claim — are prohibited and treated as a compliance violation.

24.2. **Claim splitting** — dividing a single expense into multiple smaller claims to stay under an approval or receipt threshold — is prohibited.

24.3. **Altered receipts** — any receipt that has been edited, whited-out, or otherwise tampered with — are treated as fraud, not a processing error.

24.4. **Double reimbursement** — claiming an expense that has already been reimbursed by a client, another employer, or under any other Konkan Digital benefit — is prohibited.

24.5. Finance conducts periodic random and risk-based audits of submitted claims. A confirmed violation of §24.1–24.4 may result in recovery of the amount paid, disciplinary action up to and including termination, and referral to law enforcement where fraud is indicated.
