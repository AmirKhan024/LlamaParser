# Company Profile: Konkan Digital Systems Private Limited

**Brand name:** Konkan Digital
**Sector:** Information technology and IT-enabled services (software engineering,
cloud consulting, managed support)
**Incorporated:** Mumbai, Maharashtra, India
**Headquarters:** Nariman Point, Mumbai
**Other offices:** Bengaluru (Whitefield), Pune (Hinjawadi), Delhi NCR (Gurugram)
**Headcount:** ~800 employees
**Reporting currency:** Indian Rupee (INR)
**Financial year:** 1 April – 31 March

Konkan Digital builds and supports software for clients across banking,
retail, and logistics. Roughly a third of staff travel domestically in a
given quarter for client work, and a smaller group travels internationally
(mainly North America, Western Europe, and Singapore) for client visits,
conferences, and training — which is why the expense policy prices both INR
and foreign-currency (USD/EUR/GBP) claims.
